Privacy

  • Photographs are sent directly from your browser to Pollinations (gen.pollinations.ai) for processing.
  • This app has no backend server of its own — it cannot and does not store your photographs anywhere.
  • Editing history and your API key are stored locally in this browser only.
  • Avoid uploading confidential material if you are not comfortable with third-party AI processing.
  • Pollinations' own terms and privacy policy govern how they process requests sent to their API.
